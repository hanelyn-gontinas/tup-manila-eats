MY FOOD PICKS

Places worth trying.

These are three food places near TUP Manila that I personally enjoy. I chose them because they are affordable, convenient, and perfect for students who want a good meal without spending too much.

01

Mami Karenderya

Budget-friendly Filipino food near TUP Manila.

I like eating at Mami Karenderya because the food is simple, filling, and affordable. It is one of my choices when I want a quick meal after class. I also like that I can enjoy a satisfying meal without spending too much.

₱ Budget-friendly 📍 Near TUP

02

Seddie Carinderya

Filipino carinderia in Ermita.

Seddie Carinderya is one of my favorite places to eat with my girlfriend. We usually come here at night to eat barbecue because it is tasty and affordable. The place is simple and comfortable, which makes our time together feel more special. Even though it is just a simple meal, eating here with her feels romantic and memorable.

₱ Budget-friendly 📍 Ermita

03

Alia's Goto

Affordable goto and comfort food near TUP Manila.

I would recommend Alia's Goto, This is one of my favorite places to eat because of their delicious lugaw. I often come here, especially when I want something warm and filling. Their lugaw tastes even better with chili oil. For me, even just lugaw with chili oil is already enough. It is simple, affordable, and one of my favorite meals. I enjoy eating goto here because it is a simple meal that feels perfect before a tiring day

₱ Budget-friendly 📍 Near TUP
