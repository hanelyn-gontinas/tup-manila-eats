ABOUT THE GUIDE

Hi, I'm Hanelyn C. Gontiñas.

I'm a TUP Manila student who enjoys discovering affordable and satisfying places to eat near campus.

I created TUP Eats because I love finding good and affordable food near campus. When I find a place that I really like, I enjoy sharing it with other students who may also be looking for a good meal.

I chose affordable food near TUP Manila as the focus of this guide because students often need meals that are convenient, satisfying, and easy on the budget.

My recommendations come from places that I personally enjoy. I chose them based on my own experiences, especially their food, affordability, convenience, and the memories I associate with each place.

Through TUP Eats, I want other students to discover delicious food without spending too much. I also want them to experience meals that can make them feel like a king, even on a student budget.
